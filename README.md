# Sweat Type

A type specimen for Interdot, a dot typeface, whose dots congeal: hover the letters and the dots fuse into geometric beads and bridges, grow in runs along the strokes, or turn into a heat map.

## Run it

1. Double-click **Start.command**. It starts the local server and opens the app in your browser.
2. Click the canvas and type.

Exports (PNG, SVG) and recordings (MP4, or WebM) save into this folder, in `png/`, `svg/` and `mp4/`.

## Using it

- **Text:** typeface (Interdot, the system faces, or Open font…, all set on Interdot's dot grid), dot weight, size, leading, tracking, alignment and margins.
- **Congeal:** reach and bridges, bridge width, hover grow and pull, and Random / Restart for clusters or swelling runs.
- **Cursor:** congeal on hover or press, place dots, or dry them again.
- **Material:** Sweat, Ink or Heatmap, with the text, congealed and canvas colours, or the heat map's colours and bands.
- **Canvas & output:** aspect ratio, Export PNG, Export SVG. Record is in the top bar.

Every slider's value can be typed, and every slider can bounce or loop from the round button beside its name. H hides the controls, F goes full screen, Space pauses, R restarts, G congeals at random.
